Single Shot Espresso

A single shot of espresso is approximately 1 oz (30 ml) of pure coffee perfection. The foundation of many coffee drinks.

Double Shot Espresso

A double shot of espresso delivers 2 oz (60 ml) of rich, concentrated coffee. Perfect for those who need an extra kick.

Ristretto

A ristretto is a "restricted" shot of espresso, using the same amount of coffee but half the water, resulting in a sweeter, more concentrated flavor.

Classic Latte

A classic latte combines espresso with steamed milk and a light layer of foam. Smooth, creamy, and perfectly balanced.

Vanilla Latte

Our vanilla latte adds a touch of sweet vanilla syrup to the classic recipe, creating a comforting and aromatic experience.

Caramel Latte

Rich caramel syrup meets espresso and steamed milk, topped with caramel drizzle for an indulgent treat.

Traditional Cappuccino

Equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and milk foam create the perfect cappuccino. A true Italian classic.

Dry Cappuccino

A dry cappuccino features more foam and less steamed milk, resulting in a lighter, airier drink with a stronger coffee flavor.

Wet Cappuccino

A wet cappuccino has more steamed milk and less foam, creating a creamier texture similar to a latte.

Hot Americano

Espresso shots topped with hot water, creating a coffee similar in strength to drip coffee but with a different flavor profile.

Iced Americano

Espresso shots poured over ice and topped with cold water. Refreshing and bold, perfect for warm days.

Long Black

Hot water poured first, then espresso shots on top. This method preserves the crema and delivers a stronger flavor.

Dark Mocha

Rich dark chocolate combined with espresso and steamed milk, topped with whipped cream. A chocolate lover's dream.

White Mocha

Sweet white chocolate sauce blended with espresso and steamed milk, finished with whipped cream.

Peppermint Mocha

A festive favorite combining chocolate, peppermint syrup, espresso, and steamed milk, topped with whipped cream and crushed peppermint.
